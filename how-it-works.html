How Task-Window Works

From your first task request to the final handover, we keep the process simple, transparent, and easy to follow.

01

Submit Your Task

Tell us what you need. Add your task details, requirements, deadline, and preferred budget so we understand your request clearly.

02

We Review Your Request

Our team reviews your requirements and determines how Task-Window can best help you with the work.

03

Our Employee Contacts You

Our team contacts you through your email or WhatsApp number to clarify requirements and confirm the scope, price, and expected timeline.

04

Work Begins

Once the task details are confirmed, our assigned employee starts working on your request and keeps communication clear when needed.

05

Make the Payment

When the task is completed, we inform you and provide the agreed payment instructions. Larger projects may require an advance payment before work begins.

06

Receive Your Completed Work

After payment is confirmed, Task-Window hands over the completed work to you through the appropriate delivery method.

PAYMENT & DELIVERY

Clear terms. No surprises.

We confirm the scope, price, and timeline with you before the work begins. For completed tasks, payment is made before the final deliverables are handed over.
